How This Is Gonna Work

  1. 5 Games - 4 rounds and one bonus round if there's a tie or for fun

  2. Each team plays a round, you choose one of the first 4 games

  3. One point for getting the four connected words, one point for correctly identifying the connection

  4. For the last four words you get Two points for correctly identifying the connection

  5. Two tries to identify the connection

  6. The game tells you when you're one answer off, you can shuffle the words

  7. No stealing from the other team
